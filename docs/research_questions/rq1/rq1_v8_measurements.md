Measures the **ramp-up** side of the coordination gap. ## 7. Synthesis

| Claim (from experiment plan §Hypothesis)                                      | Verdict                                                                                       | Evidence                                                                             |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Reaction latency increases monotonically with polling interval                 | **⚠️ Survivor-biased** — true for detected events, but Poll-30s misses most breaches | Push 15/15 events detected; Poll-30s 8 total (2 runs nearly blind)                   |
| Blind spot rate is zero for Push/Poll-5s, rises at Poll-12s, peaks at Poll-30s | **✅ Confirmed — strongest signal** | M6: Push 0%, Poll-5s 0%, Poll-12s 25.3%, Poll-30s **67.9%** blind spot rate |
| Throughput gap widens with polling interval                                    | **⚠️ Worst-case risk, not mean degradation**                                          | Poll-30s T2 at 37,930 vs Push min 65,551                                             |
| Service quality degrades monotonically (timeout rate, p95 latency)             | **✅ Confirmed at the tail**                                                            | Poll-30s p95 13.5 s vs ~7–8 s all others; Push σ_timeout = 0.4% vs Poll σ = 3–5% |
| Controller overhead is flat — delivery mechanism is not a resource concern    | **✅ Rule-out confirmed**                                                               | CPU 7.2–10.4%, RAM 76–78 MB                                                        |
| G8 passes for all 12 runs                                                      | **✅ Confirmed**                                                                        | All 12 PASS                                                                          |

**Thesis framing**: The system is robust enough that most runs succeed regardless of mode. The difference between telemetry cadences is not whether the system eventually copes — it's **how reliably** it copes and **how many users are affected** during the detection gap. The evidence supports a **reliability argument**: faster telemetry doesn't make the system faster on average; it makes it fail less often and less severely when conditions are hardest.
